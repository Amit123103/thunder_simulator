Cinematic Lightning & Terrain Simulation

A complete 3D interactive graphics simulation featuring volumetric storm clouds, procedural mountain crater deformation, camera-facing ribbon lightning, and HDR bloom post-processing.

# Run Locally
git clone https://github.com/Amit123103/thunder_simulator.git
python main.py
⚡

Stepped Leader Propagation

Downward cloud-to-earth lightning leader animation with camera-facing connected 3D ribbon billboards and triple-layer plasma corona shaders.

☁️

Volumetric Storm Clouds

3D FBM procedural noise raymarching with dual Henyey-Greenstein silver-lining scattering and internal flash volume illumination.

🌋

Procedural Crater Deformation

Real-time ground excavation physics, molten lava heat emission maps, carbon scorch marks, and expanding kinetic shockwave rings.

✨

HDR & Pyramid Bloom

Multi-pass high-dynamic-range rendering pipeline with ACES filmic tonemapping, wet rock specular reflections, and chromatic aberration.

🔊

Procedural 3D Audio

Dynamic spatial audio synthesis for high-voltage electrical crackles, kinetic ground blasts, and distance-delayed thunder rumbles.

🎥

Slow Mountain Auto-Orbit

Automated cinematic slow camera orbit motion with interactive ImGui telemetry HUD controls and export tools.

Built with Python, ModernGL, PyGLM, and GLSL Shaders. View code on GitHub.
